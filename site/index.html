Quick setup

Ready to dictate in four steps

Takes about five minutes — mostly downloading the model once. A guided onboarding window walks you through every step on first launch.

  1. Install Frazia

    Open the DMG and drag Frazia to Applications, or run the Homebrew command in Terminal. Launch Frazia from Applications to see its waveform icon in the menu bar.

    Requires a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 13 Ventura or later.

    Applications
    or via Homebrew Cask · brew install --cask frazia

  2. Allow Microphone & Accessibility

    Grant access once in System Settings → Privacy & Security, then test your voice level in Microphone (keep Auto until the meter hits the green zone).

    • Microphone Captures speech only while you press or hold ⌥ Space. Allowed
    • Accessibility Finds the active text caret and inserts recognized text. Allowed

  3. Download the model for your Mac

    In Recognition, keep On this Mac and download the model badged Recommended for your Mac. Frazia selects it automatically from your chip and memory.

    Medium and Tiny are also available, or connect Deepgram, OpenAI, or Groq with your own Keychain-stored API key.

  4. Press ⌥ Space and speak

    Place your cursor in any text field, press ⌥ Option + Space, speak, and press ⌥ Space again (or hold and release). Change the shortcut anytime in Settings → Shortcuts.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Frazia really free? Do I need an account or API key?

Yes — completely free, with no account or API key required. The default “On this Mac” engine runs offline after downloading a Whisper model. Cloud engines are optional.

Why does Frazia ask for Accessibility permission?

macOS requires Accessibility for any utility that locates the active text caret and inserts text into another app’s window. Without it, you can still dictate and copy results from History.

Text didn’t appear after dictating. How do I fix it?

Verify Frazia is enabled in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. If it is already on after an app update, open Frazia Settings → General and click Reset & Request Again…. If you switched windows or focused a password field, Frazia intentionally holds insertion — press ⌥ Shift V to paste or copy it from History.

Which Whisper model should I pick?

Pick the model marked “Recommended for your Mac”. On Apple Silicon with 16 GB+ RAM, that is Large v3 Turbo. On 8 GB Macs, Frazia recommends Small for Russian and multilingual speech, or Base for English.

Are my voice recordings stored anywhere?

Not by default. “Save dictations” is set to “Off — memory only”, so audio lives in RAM during recognition and is immediately erased. Optional 24-hour or 7-day History encrypts audio using a key in your macOS Keychain.

Can I use Deepgram, OpenAI, or Groq instead of offline Whisper?

Yes. Deepgram streams text while you speak, while OpenAI and Groq transcribe when you stop. You supply your own API key (stored in macOS Keychain) and pay the provider directly.

How is Frazia different from built-in macOS Dictation?

Frazia runs open Whisper models up to Large v3 Turbo locally across 14 languages, floats cleanly beside the caret without stealing focus, supports spoken formatting and Shift-to-send, protects against wrong-window insertion, and offers optional cloud engines.

How do I update or uninstall Frazia?

Quit Frazia and run brew upgrade --cask frazia, or drag the new Frazia.app into Applications and click Replace — your permissions, models, and settings stay saved. Always launch Frazia from Applications, not the mounted DMG. To uninstall, quit Frazia and move it to the Trash.
